01

Narrative and positioning strategy

The foundation of everything else. We define the category you want to lead, the point of view you will defend, and the evidence that backs it up.

  • Category and audience definition, mapped to the roles in your buying group
  • Your point of view, including what you challenge in your market
  • Proof pillars: the stories, data and frameworks that make you credible
  • A messaging hierarchy you can reuse in sales, hiring and fundraising

You receivea Narrative Blueprint, a message house and a 90-day content map.

Why it mattersClear positioning gives buyers, partners and candidates one consistent story about what you stand for.

02

Founder interview engine

Generic content comes from templates. Authority comes from experience. Every two weeks we interview you for about 45 minutes and turn what you say into an organised library of stories, opinions, frameworks and answers to the objections your buyers raise.

You receivea question bank tailored to your market, transcripts, an insight library and a voice guide that keeps everything sounding like you.

Why it mattersYour expertise becomes a reusable library, so content never starts from a blank page.

03

LinkedIn thought leadership

LinkedIn is where most B2B buyers form a first impression of a founder. We write, design and schedule your content, and support the conversation around it. LinkedIn is one channel within a wider system, not the whole product.

  • Profile and headline rewrite built around your narrative
  • Text posts, carousels and visual formats drawn from your interviews
  • A posting calendar and a comment strategy for the people who matter in your market

You receive12 to 16 approved posts a month, depending on your plan.

Why it mattersConsistent, credible visibility keeps you familiar to the people who will one day need you.

04

Long-form and owned media

Posts spark recognition. Long-form builds depth. We produce newsletters, articles, point-of-view reports and case narratives that live on your own channels and keep working long after a post has scrolled away. Long-form is part of the Authority Engine, while the 30-day sprint focuses on LinkedIn. The mix of formats is agreed with you at kickoff.

Why it mattersDepth builds understanding that a single post cannot, and the pieces keep working on channels you own.

05

Authority distribution

Your thinking should travel beyond your own feed. We position you for podcasts, panels, guest articles and collaborations with complementary experts, and we turn your best content into sales enablement such as point-of-view one-pagers and email sequences. We prepare the pitches and the positioning, and the scope is agreed with you. We can support these opportunities, but we cannot guarantee acceptance, placement, reach or resulting business.

Why it mattersYour best thinking reaches people who are not yet following you.

06

Search and AI visibility: SEO, AEO and GEO

Buyers now ask search engines and AI assistants to explain categories, compare providers and recommend experts. We write your thinking so those systems can understand and cite it. In plain English: SEO (search engine optimisation) helps people find you in search results. AEO (answer engine optimisation) structures content so that it answers questions directly. GEO (generative engine optimisation) makes it more likely that AI assistants draw on and reference your work. No one can guarantee rankings or AI citations, and we do not.

  • Keyword and question research for your category
  • Answer-first article structure, FAQs and schema markup
  • Consistent descriptions of you and your firm across your site, LinkedIn and external profiles
  • Citation-ready data, clear definitions and named authorship
  • Regular checks on how AI assistants describe your firm

Why it mattersWhen buyers research your category, your expertise is easier to find and easier to understand.

07

Measurement and reporting

A monthly report covers reach and engagement quality, inbound conversations, qualified calls, branded search and mentions in AI answers, along with what we learned and what we recommend next. A quarterly narrative review keeps your position sharp. We agree the measures that matter to your business at kickoff, and the findings shape the next month’s content.

Why it mattersYou can see what is working, and each cycle gets sharper.
