About

Credibility is built in public, with evidence.

In B2B, trust is the product. Founders who explain their thinking clearly, show their work and stay consistent earn it faster than those who simply advertise. Northbound Consultancy exists to help expert-led businesses do that well.

Why we exist

Why Northbound exists

Most founder content is generic: templated posts, recycled advice and a calendar to fill. It rarely reflects what the founder actually knows, so it rarely earns trust.

Northbound exists to change that. We interview founders, extract their real experience, opinions and evidence, and turn it into content they approve and are proud to publish. We are a strategic, human-led partner for founder authority, not a volume-based ghostwriting service.

Our principles

What we stand for

01

Substance over volume.

One idea that changes how a buyer sees their problem is worth more than ten posts that repeat the obvious.

02

Evidence over adjectives.

We prefer a sourced number or a real story to a claim that merely sounds impressive.

03

Your voice, always.

We interview, draft and refine. The experience and the opinions are yours.

04

Authority over reach.

We build a position that lasts, not a spike that fades.
