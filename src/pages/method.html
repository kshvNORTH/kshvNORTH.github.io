The method

The Northbound Method

A four-stage framework for turning a founder’s expertise into category authority.

Most content programmes start with a calendar. Ours starts with a position. The Northbound Method moves from deciding what you should be known for, to capturing the experience that proves it, to publishing it consistently, to measuring and compounding what works.

  1. Stage 1

    Stage 1: Position (weeks 1 to 2)

    Decide what to own.

    • We audit your current presence and the narratives your competitors tell.
    • We define your category, your audience and the roles in their buying group.
    • We choose your point of view, including what you challenge in your market.
    • We select the proof pillars (stories, data, frameworks) that make you credible.

    You receivea Narrative Blueprint, a message house and a 90-day content map.

  2. Stage 2

    Stage 2: Extract (from week 2, every two weeks)

    Capture what only you know.

    • Structured 45-minute interviews draw out your stories, decisions, mistakes, frameworks and answers to buyer objections.
    • Everything is organised into an insight library that you own.
    • Your voice guide is refined after every cycle, so the writing sounds more like you over time.

    You receivetranscripts, an insight library and a living voice guide.

  3. Stage 3

    Stage 3: Publish (from week 3)

    Turn insight into a steady body of work.

    • We build a monthly calendar across LinkedIn, newsletter and long-form content.
    • Every draft starts from your own words, and every claim is checked and sourced.
    • You review each draft and either approve it or send comments, and we revise it. Nothing goes live without your approval, and if feedback is delayed the publishing schedule moves with it.
    • We adapt language, terminology and communication to your audience and industry.
    • We publish and support the conversation around each piece.

    You receive12 to 16 approved LinkedIn posts a month, plus long-form content and visuals depending on your plan.

  4. Stage 4

    Stage 4: Compound (monthly and quarterly)

    Build on what works.

    • A monthly report shows what is moving the right people, including inbound conversations, qualified calls, branded search and AI mentions, with what we learned and what we recommend next.
    • We double down on the topics that start the right conversations.
    • We optimise your content for search engines and AI answers (SEO, AEO and GEO).
    • We turn your best-performing content into sales enablement.
    • A quarterly narrative review keeps your position sharp as your market changes.

    You receivea monthly report and a quarterly narrative review.
