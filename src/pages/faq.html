Frequently asked questions

What is founder-led content?

Founder-led content is content built from a founder’s own experience, opinions and evidence and published under their name to build trust and authority with B2B buyers. It differs from brand content because its credibility comes from a named person rather than a logo.

What is a founder-led content agency?

A founder-led content agency interviews founders and experts, then turns their knowledge into strategic content published under the founder’s name. Northbound also builds the narrative, distribution and search and AI visibility around that content.

How is Northbound different from a LinkedIn ghostwriter?

A ghostwriter typically writes posts. Northbound starts with positioning and a point of view, builds every piece from interviews with you, publishes across LinkedIn, long-form and search, and reports on pipeline signals as well as impressions. You approve everything before it goes live.

How much of my time does it take?

About 45 minutes every two weeks for an interview, plus a few minutes to review and approve content. We handle drafting, design, scheduling and reporting.

How long before we see results?

Founder-led content is a compounding channel, so we don’t promise specific figures. Expect early signals, such as engagement from the right people, profile visits and first conversations, within the first two months, and a more meaningful effect on pipeline over three to six months. We agree measurable targets with you at kickoff.

What are AEO and GEO, and why should founders care?

Answer engine optimisation (AEO) structures content so that it answers questions directly. Generative engine optimisation (GEO) aims to make content more likely to be cited in AI-generated answers. They matter because buyers increasingly use AI assistants to research categories and providers: in Edelman and LinkedIn’s 2026 study, 82% of B2B decision-makers said thought leadership is becoming more valuable as AI becomes a common source of business answers.

Do you work with consultants and coaches?

Yes. We work with consulting and advisory boutiques, and with coaches who sell to organisations or senior leaders and have a defined offer.

Do I need to have published content before?

No. Many founders we work with have never published consistently. The interviews do the heavy lifting, and we draft from what you say.

I don’t have time to write. Is this still workable?

Yes. The main time commitment is about 45 minutes every two weeks for an interview, plus reviewing drafts. We do the writing, and you approve everything.

How do you capture my voice?

Every piece starts from your own interview. We build a voice guide from your words and refine it after each cycle, so drafts sound like you rather than like a template.

Can you work with more than one founder or leader?

Yes. Our Narrative partnership is designed for firms with several leaders who each need a distinct voice within one shared narrative.

Do you guarantee leads, followers or rankings?

No. We do not guarantee leads, revenue, follower counts, search rankings, AI citations or media placements. We focus on building credible, consistent authority and measuring what it does.

How much does it cost?

An authority audit starts from $1,500. A 30-day founder content sprint starts from $2,000. The Authority Engine starts from $3,000 a month with a three-month minimum. Narrative partnerships for multi-leader firms are scoped individually.

Will the content sound like me?

Yes. Every piece starts from your own interview, your voice guide is refined after each cycle, and nothing is published until you approve it.

Who owns the content?

You do. You own the content we create for you and the insight library built from your interviews, and we confirm this in your agreement.

How do you measure thought leadership?

Our monthly report tracks inbound conversations, qualified calls, branded search, engagement from the roles and firms you want to reach, and mentions in AI answers. Impressions are included for context but are not the goal.

Do you use AI to write the content?

We use AI tools to support research, structure and editing. Every piece starts from your interview, is written and edited by people, and is fact-checked and approved by you. We do not publish text that contains invented experience.
