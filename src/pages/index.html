The gap

Your expertise is your advantage. Your market can’t see it yet.

Most B2B founders understand their category better than anyone writing about it. But that knowledge lives in sales calls, client sessions and their own heads, while the market hears from louder voices with less to say.

Founder-led content closes that gap. It puts your real experience, opinions and evidence in front of the people who will one day need you, consistently enough that you are the name that comes to mind when they do.

The evidence

Why founder-led content works now

95%

Research by Professor John Dawes of the Ehrenberg-Bass Institute, published with LinkedIn’s B2B Institute, finds that at any given moment only about 5% of B2B buyers are in the market. The other 95% will buy later, and they will buy from the names they already know.

Source: Dawes, Ehrenberg-Bass / LinkedIn B2B Institute ↗
73%

In Edelman and LinkedIn’s 2024 study, about 73% of B2B decision-makers said an organisation’s thought leadership is a more trustworthy basis for judging its capabilities than its marketing materials and product sheets.

Source: Edelman × LinkedIn, 2024 ↗
82%

In the 2026 edition of the same study, 82% of B2B decision-makers said thought leadership is becoming more valuable as AI becomes a common source of business answers.

Source: Edelman × LinkedIn, 2026 ↗

Buyers research you before they ever speak to you: through people, posts and now AI assistants. What they find is your first sales conversation.

Our standards

Credibility through words and actions

For a founder, putting your name on content is a trust decision. These are the standards we hold ourselves to.

  • Your voice, your experience.Every piece starts with your own words from an interview. We never invent anecdotes, clients or numbers.

  • You approve everything.Nothing is published without your sign-off.

  • Every claim is sourced.Statistics are attributed to their original source, and we tell you when we can’t verify something.

  • Honest measurement.We report pipeline signals such as inbound conversations, qualified calls and share of voice, not only impressions. We don’t promise follower counts.

  • Confidential by default.Client details and unpublished work stay private unless you choose otherwise.

Fit

Who Northbound is for

A strong fit if you:

  • are a B2B founder, partner or principal whose reputation influences who buys;

Probably not a fit if you want:
