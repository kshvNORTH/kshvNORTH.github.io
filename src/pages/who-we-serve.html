Who we serve

Who Northbound serves

Northbound works with B2B founders and expert-led firms whose personal credibility influences who buys. If people choose you because they trust your judgment, founder-led content is one of the most direct ways to earn that trust at scale.

01

B2B SaaS and technology founders

The challenge

Crowded categories, similar features and buyers who compare you before they ever speak to you.

What we build

A point of view on the problem your category exists to solve, so you are known as the voice of that category, not one more vendor in it.

The outcome

A founder recognised by buyers, candidates and investors before the first conversation.

02

Consulting and advisory boutiques

The challenge

The founder is the product, and growth depends on referrals and the founder’s limited time.

What we build

Content that demonstrates your judgment before the first call, so prospects arrive already convinced of your expertise.

The outcome

A more predictable flow of inbound conversations that do not rely on one person’s network.

03

Coaches and expert-led firms

The challenge

Testimonials alone rarely set a premium offer apart, and generic advice blends into the noise.

What we build

A clear niche, a distinctive method and evidence of how you think, expressed in your own voice.

The outcome

Credibility that supports premium pricing. We work with coaches who sell to organisations or senior leaders and have a defined offer.

Is it a fit?

Who we work best with

We work best with founders who have something meaningful to say, a business worth building and the willingness to make their expertise visible.

Northbound is best suited to founders with genuine expertise and a defined offer. Not every founder is an automatic fit, and we will say so honestly on a strategy call if we are not the right partner.
